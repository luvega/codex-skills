# Chemical Structure Rules

Use this when reviewing chemical structures, reaction schemes, mechanism diagrams, and ChemDraw-derived panels.

## General

- Start from Nature-style ChemDraw settings when possible.
- Keep bond lengths, bond angles, and bond thicknesses consistent across the manuscript.
- Use discrete bonds rather than ring shortcuts except where chemically conventional.
- Do not use bold bonds for emphasis because they can be confused with stereochemical notation; use color sparingly if emphasis is necessary.

## Atom Labels and Abbreviations

- Label only heteroatoms or groups changed during a reaction when possible.
- Use `Me` for methyl groups attached to heteroatoms; use unlabeled bonds for methyl groups attached to carbon when clear.
- Define non-standard abbreviations.
- Avoid ambiguous abbreviations in the middle of chains.
- Put charges on the formally charged atom and use the charge tool rather than text.
- Show salts with counterions in dissociated form.
- Use superscript numbers for R groups, not subscripts.

## Stereochemistry and Organometallics

- Specify stereochemistry with boldface and dashed wedges.
- Omit hydrogens unless needed to specify stereochemistry.
- Use wedges, not thick bonds, for stereochemistry.
- Check chirality after redrawing, rotating, or flipping structures.
- For organometallics, keep notation for complex charge and counterions consistent.

## Arrows

- Use purpose-specific arrows for retrosynthesis, multistep synthesis, mechanisms, resonance, and equilibrium.
- In mechanisms, draw curly arrows from the center of a breaking bond or lone electron pair to the center of a forming bond or target atom.

## Nature Drawing Settings

- Atom-label font: Arial or Helvetica, 6 pt.
- Caption text: Arial or Helvetica, 6 or 6.5 pt.
- Fixed length: 0.381 cm.
- Bond spacing: 18% of length.
- Line width: 0.021 cm.
- Bold width: 0.055 cm.
- Margin width: 0.042 cm.
- Hash spacing: 0.06 cm.
- Chain angle: 120 degrees.
- Absolute minimum atom-label size for exceptional scaled structures: 5 pt.

Use boldface only for compound numbers or descriptors. Use italics only when chemically or typographically conventional.
