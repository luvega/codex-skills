# Review And Research Framework

Use this for narrative reviews, mini-reviews, grant background, topic maps, and literature discussion sections.

## Framework Output

Produce:

1. `Scope`: population/system, disease, method class, time window, and exclusions.
2. `Taxonomy axes`: 2-4 axes such as modality, method, biological question, validation level, or translational stage.
3. `Evidence matrix`: claim, evidence source, status, and limitations.
4. `Controversy map`: disagreements, unresolved assumptions, negative evidence, or incompatible models.
5. `Knowledge gaps`: missing datasets, missing validation, weak benchmarks, or mechanistic gaps.
6. `Candidate figures`: taxonomy matrix, mechanism schematic, workflow diagram, evidence ladder, or benchmark table.
7. `Future directions`: specific analyses or experiments, not generic "more research is needed".

## Review Writing Gate

- Do not summarize papers one by one unless the section is a methods inventory.
- Group literature by problem, assumption, method, evidence strength, or controversy.
- Every strong review conclusion needs a literature or data locator.
- Mark inferred synthesis as synthesis, not as a cited author claim.
