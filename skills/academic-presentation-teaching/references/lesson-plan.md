# Lesson Plan

Use lesson plans for courses, tutorials, journal clubs, and lab training.

Required elements:

- `audience`: learner background and prerequisite knowledge.
- `topic`: one teaching topic.
- `learning_objectives`: observable outcomes.
- `session_plan`: timed sequence of teaching activities.
- `case_studies`: paper, dataset, figure, or workflow examples.
- `discussion_questions`: prompts that reveal misconceptions.
- `assessment_prompts`: short tasks, quizzes, or rewrite exercises.
- `evidence_map`: claims and sources.

Teaching rules:

- Start from a concrete case before abstraction.
- Separate concept, method, result, and limitation.
- Use learner actions: identify, compare, interpret, justify, revise.
- Include at least one misconception check for AI models, causal claims, or biological mechanism.
